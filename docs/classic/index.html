What it does

Everything a one-person publishing operation needs, in one login.

Not a bundle of ten products with ten bills. One workspace where the writing, the making and the scheduling share the same idea.

Write once, adapt everywhere

One idea becomes a caption, a thread, a full article, a carousel and a short. Edit any of them before anything ships.

A queue you can audit

Every run stores the text it used, the channel it targeted and the exact response. No black box, no mystery posts.

Keys you keep

Third-party tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Passwords are hashed with scrypt, never stored in the clear.

Made on the device

Carousels export as PNG, video and shorts record as WebM in your browser. Files never touch the server.

Under the hood

Free because the expensive half runs on your machine.

The generator, the keyword engine and the answer-engine checker are deterministic local code. Carousels and video are rendered by the browser, not by a render farm.

That removes the per-generation bill that forces every other tool into a subscription.

  • ✓No model bill. Swap in your own API key whenever you want a different voice — it stays encrypted and optional.
  • ✓No render farm. A 1080×1350 deck or a 9:16 short is drawn frame by frame on your device.
  • ✓No advertising. There is no third-party analytics script and nothing to upgrade to.
