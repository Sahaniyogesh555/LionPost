I stopped scheduling posts by hand and got 6 hours back

AR Alex Rivera · Oct 20, 2026 · 180 views

I used to keep a spreadsheet of captions, a notes app full of links, and a calendar reminder that I ignored. Last month I moved all of it into one queue.

The setup is boring on purpose:

  • One topic per week, written down before Monday
  • A reusable caption shape: hook, three useful lines, one ask
  • One run of the queue each morning, ten minutes, no scrolling

The part that mattered was not the tool. It was deciding, in advance, what the week was about. Automation only helps after that decision exists.

What does your queue look like? I am still looking for a better way to handle images.
