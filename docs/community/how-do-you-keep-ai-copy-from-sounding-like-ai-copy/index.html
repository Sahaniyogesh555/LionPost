How do you keep AI copy from sounding like AI copy?

PN Priya Nair · Oct 17, 2026 · 454 views

I use a generator for first drafts, then edit hard. The pattern that finally worked for me:

  • Delete the first paragraph. It is almost always a warm-up.
  • Replace every abstract noun with a thing I could photograph.
  • Read it out loud. If I would not say the sentence to a friend, it goes.
  • Keep one specific number. Specifics are the loudest signal of a human behind the text.

Curious what others do. Is there a prompt that reliably gets you closer on the first pass?
