What I learned publishing 30 articles in 30 days

DA Diego Alvarez · Oct 19, 2026 · 317 views

Thirty articles in thirty days. Here is the honest report.

What worked

  1. A narrow topic. Every article answered a question I had already heard twice.
  2. An outline before the draft. Always. The days I skipped it produced the weakest pieces.
  3. One call to action, not four.

What did not

  • Publishing before proofreading. Three posts needed quiet fixes afterwards.
  • Chasing a keyword I had no chance of ranking for. It brought 40 visits and no subscribers.
  • Writing about tools instead of writing about problems.

If you are starting the same challenge: pick twelve topics first, not thirty. You will thank yourself in week three.
