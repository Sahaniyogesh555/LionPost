Ask a question
Is LionPost really free?

Yes. Every tool is free, with no card and no trial. The heavy AI features work offline on a built-in engine. If you want to use your own Gemini key, you can add one in settings and it is stored encrypted.

What can it actually do?

Write captions, threads and long articles; design carousels and pins; render vertical video; research keywords; score a page for answer engines; read your RSS feed and queue every new item; and schedule all of it across the channels you connect.

Do I need one account for everything?

Yes, and that is the point. One login opens the studio, the scheduler, the research tools and the community.

Where do my videos go?

Nowhere. They are rendered in your browser and downloaded straight to your device. The server only stores the script if you ask it to.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, sessions are HTTP-only cookies, and third-party tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Nothing posts unless you set it up.

What happens when a connected account fails?

The run is marked failed with the exact response, the automation keeps its schedule, and you can retry from the run log.
