About

Built for the person doing all of it.

LionPost started because publishing alone means being the writer, the designer, the scheduler and the analyst, usually in the same hour. The tools were never the hard part. The context switching was.

Want the older layout? The same site is also available as a classic single-page theme.

What this is

An independent workspace: sixteen tools under one login, plus a forum where the people using them compare notes. 6 members, 10 threads, 24 runs logged.

What it will not do

  • It will not post anywhere you have not connected.
  • It will not invent quotes, reviews or statistics to fill a draft.
  • It will not upload your video. Rendering happens in your browser.
  • It will not pretend a dry run reached a platform.

How it is built

A small Node service, SQLite, server-rendered pages and vanilla JavaScript. No bundler, no tracking scripts, no third-party analytics. The content engine is deterministic and runs offline; add your own Gemini key if you want a second opinion.
